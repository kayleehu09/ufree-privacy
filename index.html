Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

About UFree

UFree helps friends share when they are available to hang out, study, or help each other. UFree is intended for people age 13 and older. Questions about this policy can be sent to kayleehu09@gmail.com.

Information UFree handles

  • Account information: your email address and authentication details are handled by Supabase when you create or use an account. UFree does not receive or store your password in its app database.
  • Profile information: your display name, username, and account identifier.
  • Availability: activities you share, the connection method you select, optional help category or detail, and the times those statuses are created or expire.
  • Friend and safety information: friend requests and connections, favorites, blocks, and reports you submit, including the selected reason and any details you provide.
  • App activity: nudges and help offers you send, which can appear to the recipient in UFree’s Activity area for up to 24 hours.
  • Date of birth: signup asks for a date of birth to check the 13+ minimum. The check happens on your device; UFree does not send or save the date of birth.

UFree does not ask for or use your precise location. “In person” describes a connection method and does not share a place.

How information is used and shared

UFree uses this information to create and secure accounts, show profile and availability information to accepted friends, manage friend requests and safety controls, deliver in-app activity, respond to support requests, and maintain the service.

UFree uses Supabase for account authentication, database hosting, and related backend services. Information is processed by Supabase on UFree’s behalf under its service terms and privacy practices. UFree does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Your profile and availability are shown within UFree according to the app’s friend and visibility controls. Reports are stored so they can be reviewed. The app currently has no in-app messaging or calling.

Retention and deletion

Availability that expires or is cleared is no longer shown as active in the app. In-app nudges and help offers are available to recipients for up to 24 hours. Backend records may remain until they are removed or the account is deleted.

You can request account deletion in Settings. Deleting an account removes the associated authentication account and database records, including profile, availability, friend relationships, and related activity. The deletion service also attempts to remove the account’s stored profile-photo files. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where needed for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

You can also contact kayleehu09@gmail.com with privacy questions or a deletion request.

Security

UFree uses Supabase authentication and access controls to limit access to account data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in details private and contact us if you believe your account is being used without permission.

Children’s privacy

UFree’s signup is limited to people age 13 or older. UFree does not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to UFree, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

UFree may update this policy as the app changes. The “Last updated” date above will show when a revision takes effect. If a change materially affects how information is handled, UFree will provide notice in the app or by another appropriate means.

Contact

Email kayleehu09@gmail.com for questions or concerns about privacy.
